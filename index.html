FEATURED HISTORICAL DISCOVERY · PROTOTYPE

What happens when you follow the money?

This first Black Continuity visual-story test explores Britain’s slave-trade financial network and the compensation system that followed abolition. The purpose of this prototype is to test the storytelling experience before final historical research and source selection are installed.

SCROLL TO ENTER THE STORY ↓
01 · THE QUESTION

Abolition Ended Slavery. But Who Was Compensated?

The story begins with a question rather than a wall of text. One idea fills the screen and gives the visitor a reason to continue.

02 · SHOW HOW WE KNOW

Bring the Evidence Into the Story.

Instead of asking visitors to simply trust the narration, the finished experience can reveal registers, parliamentary records, ledgers, maps and other appropriate historical sources as the story unfolds.

03 · PEOPLE & INSTITUTIONS

Move From the System to the People Inside It.

Each verified connection can become its own visual chapter: a person, company, institution or place, followed by the evidence that supports the connection.

CONCEPTUAL RECONSTRUCTION · FINAL HISTORICAL CLAIMS TO BE VERIFIED
04 · THE TURN

The Compensation Story Changes the Question.

The completed BCPG version will distinguish documented fact, interpretation and qualification while showing what the records establish about compensation after abolition.

BCPG EVIDENCE-FIRST STORYTELLING

The Story Follows the Evidence. The Evidence Does Not Follow the Story.

In the finished Historical Discovery, every publishable historical claim will be independently researched and verified. Sources, uncertainty and qualifications will remain part of the experience rather than being hidden behind the animation.

A PLACE TO CONNECT THROUGH HISTORY

The Black Continuity community is taking shape for people who care about Black history. It will be a moderated place to meet, exchange perspectives, ask questions, share stories and disagree respectfully without harassment or abuse.

DISCUSS BLACK HISTORY

Talk about people, events, communities, books, sources and historical questions with others who share the interest.

WHAT HISTORY DO YOU WANT TO KNOW?

Members can ask about a person, place, event, community, time period, country or historical question they want explored.

WHAT SHOULD BLACK CONTINUITY EXPLORE?

Suggest subjects you believe deserve deeper attention. Community interest can inform review without automatically deciding what BCPG publishes.

WHAT BLACK STORY DO YOU WANT TO TELL?

Share a family or community story, migration, neighborhood, military experience, institution, photograph, document or memory you do not want forgotten.

DISCOVER IT. DISCUSS IT. ASK ABOUT IT. SHARE IT. PRESERVE IT.
COMMUNITY + HISTORICAL INTEGRITY

Your Voice Matters. Evidence Still Matters.

A community story, question or popular topic can be valuable without automatically becoming verified BCPG history or a publishing decision. Contributions can reveal interests and possible research opportunities; any BCPG historical or publishing use requires the appropriate review, permissions, rights and verification.
